Not by chance

An attempt to understand the information system that surrounds us

01 Tactical 02 Relational 03 Strategic

A three-dimensional epistemic framework for navigating the contemporary information system. Not a manual, not a definitive answer. A tool for pausing, looking at yourself from the outside while you read, asking who benefits from your immediate reaction.

With an example on inflation that runs through the entire document.

Written by a European. The historical examples and structural references are drawn from that context. The mechanisms, however, do not have a passport.

About Masatz

IT professional. Interested in geopolitics, privacy, and complex systems.

At a certain point I stopped reading the news. Not television. I had already left that behind. I mean the newspapers, the websites, the sources I considered serious. Then I began having a strange feeling: as if behind the individual stories there was a pattern. Not a conspiracy, nothing that precise. Something more subtle.

That awareness did not make me suspicious of everything. It made me curious about how things work. This document is the result of that work. This is a comparison, not a lesson.
