Before the First Reaction

Source, date, context — before I pass it on.

Some messages do not just inform me.

They change my pace.

Some things I try to notice:
  • Urgency before context.
  • A source I cannot reach.
  • A request to share immediately.
  • A story that is still moving.
  • A frame with too few alternatives.

Not to decide whether a message is true or false.

But to notice when it gives me very little time, very little context, or very few alternatives before asking me to react.

If I cannot check, I can at least avoid adding speed.
